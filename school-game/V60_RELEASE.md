# Geometry Tactical Classroom V60

V60 preserves the complete V59 classroom game and focuses on two teacher-requested corrections: clearer Thales labels and a controlled pause policy.

## Thales and similar right triangles

- Every figure remains a right triangle.
- Both the large and small triangles display a 90-degree marker.
- Vertices A, B, C, D and E are identified directly on the figure.
- AD, AB, AE and AC use compact callout labels with leader lines.
- Labels are clamped inside the figure area and separated from the instructional panel.
- The panel explicitly states that A-D-B and A-E-C are collinear and that DE is parallel to BC.

## Mission timing

- Total mission time is 30 minutes.
- A team may use at most three pauses.
- Each pause lasts up to 30 seconds and is consumed as soon as it starts.
- The team may resume early.
- The mission clock and tactical simulation freeze for the actual pause duration.
- Pauses are unavailable during geometry checkpoints so answer time remains fair.
- Pause use and total paused time are included in the encrypted result record.

## Preserved V59 systems

- full-name registration and group 8°A, 8°B or 8°C;
- finite E-key weapon throws;
- unarmed melee combat and humanoid melee enemies;
- five connected rooms, dynamic glass and final boss;
- procedural right-triangle-only question bank with repeat protection;
- encrypted AES-256-GCM local results and teacher PIN 9109;
- full per-user Windows x64 installer with shortcuts and uninstall entry.

V60 can read the existing encrypted V59 vault and migrates those reports into the V60 vault on the next protected save.
